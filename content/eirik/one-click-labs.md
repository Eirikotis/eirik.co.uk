# Quantitative DeFi investment and portfolio infrastructure (One Click Labs)

## Role

- Product and Quantitative Analyst
- October 2021 to May 2024

One Click Labs worked on DeFi investment-product infrastructure. The product brought fragmented yield and market data into a common decision framework so opportunities could be compared as part of a portfolio rather than ranked by advertised APY alone.

Eirik's role sat between founders, data infrastructure, quantitative research and the user-facing product. He worked directly with founders across product definition and iteration.

## Data and technical work

- DeFiLlama and protocol API integrations;
- Python and SQL workflows;
- extraction, cleaning, normalisation and validation of market data;
- historical APY, TVL and protocol data;
- exception handling and data-quality work;
- user and product requirements translated into technical priorities.

## Concrete data-quality example

This is Eirik's clearest example of working with messy data. DeFi yield information was fragmented across DeFiLlama and individual protocol APIs. Lending pools, staking positions, liquidity pools and automated vaults exposed data in inconsistent formats; APYs changed rapidly; and historical performance was incomplete. Even when two opportunities displayed the same headline yield, their return mechanics, liquidity constraints and risk exposures could be materially different.

Eirik worked on Python and SQL workflows that extracted this protocol-level data, cleaned and normalised it into a comparable structure, validated records and handled exceptions. The resulting data layer supported historical APY and TVL analysis, risk and return features, backtesting, portfolio optimisation and the user-facing portfolio workflow. The important outcome was not merely cleaner rows: it was turning unlike and unreliable market inputs into a common decision framework.

Do not invent exchange feeds, research-report data, client inputs, interpolation or imputation as part of this example. The supported sources and issues are DeFiLlama and protocol APIs; inconsistent formats; rapidly changing APYs; incomplete history; validation and exception handling; and economically different products that could not be compared safely from headline yield alone.

## Quantitative work

- expected-return estimation;
- volatility and downside analysis;
- risk scoring;
- strategy correlations and diversification;
- portfolio optimisation and efficient-frontier analysis;
- Monte Carlo analysis;
- backtesting and portfolio comparison;
- lending-market and AMM-liquidity analysis;
- capital allocation and live strategy monitoring.

The analysis considered protocol and smart-contract risk, asset volatility, liquidity and withdrawal constraints, yield instability, incentive dependence, impermanent loss, concentration, blockchain exposure and bridge exposure.

Eirik helped shape portfolio-building workflows, translate quantitative outputs into product features, prioritise data and research work and coordinate between product, technical delivery and live investment activity.
