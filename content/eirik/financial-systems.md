# Financial-product systems

Use this compact layer when the visitor asks whether Eirik has built financial products. Lead with the credit protocol, then the quantitative DeFi investment infrastructure. Do not add unrelated products merely to lengthen the answer.

## Credit protocol for Bittensor assets

Eirik led product and commercial development for a credit protocol for Bittensor built on Morpho isolated lending markets. The system was designed to let Bittensor subnet assets support borrowing through isolated collateral markets. He defined the product and financial architecture, requirements, user flows, roadmap and acceptance criteria; designed collateral eligibility, loan-to-value and debt-cap methodology; analysed market capacity, liquidity, stressed execution and price impact; worked through oracle, bridge, router and liquidation dependencies; developed interest, fee, revenue and commercial models; and coordinated an eight-person development team plus an external technical lead. The venture was called VOID. Substantial funded product and development work reached functional implementation, but it did not complete an external fundraise, scaled launch or produce scaled TVL/revenue. Eirik did not personally write every production component.

## Quantitative DeFi investment infrastructure

At One Click Labs, Eirik contributed to financial-product and portfolio infrastructure that brought fragmented DeFi yield and market data into a common decision framework. His work covered protocol APIs, Python and SQL data pipelines, cleaning and validation, lending markets, AMM liquidity, expected returns, volatility and downside analysis, risk scoring, correlations, portfolio optimisation, Monte Carlo analysis, backtesting, capital allocation, monitoring and product economics. He worked directly with founders across product definition, quantitative research, data priorities and live investment analysis. This was shared company work rather than a product he built alone, and he does not claim ownership of the company's later Yield Network product.
