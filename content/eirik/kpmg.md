# KPMG UK

## Role

- Associate, Financial Services Assurance
- September 2024 to July 2026
- Formal employment at KPMG UK

The role gave him direct exposure to how financial institutions operate across finance, operations, risk, technology and reporting. ## Work performed

- mapped ownership, workflows, handoffs, systems and data flows;
- worked across financial processes, operational processes and technology-enabled controls;
- analysed reconciliations, reporting outputs, evidence and management information;
- tested controls and investigated exceptions;
- identified data-quality issues, failure modes and operational dependencies;
- assessed operational risk, control gaps, data integrity and potential financial exposure;
- communicated with finance, operations, risk, technology and management stakeholders;
- documented findings and supported prioritised remediation and clearer accountability.

The work can be understood as connecting client and market context, operating models, systems and data, and financial reporting. It was assurance work, but it developed broader finance-systems judgement: understanding how processes actually operate, where information moves, where dependencies sit and what happens when controls or data fail.
